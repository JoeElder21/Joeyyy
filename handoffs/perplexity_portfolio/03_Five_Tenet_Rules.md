# Five-Tenet Test

Apply all five tenets to every new position and addition. Score each `PASS`, `FAIL`, or `UNKNOWN`; no averaging or compensating strengths. A candidate proceeds only with five passes and no automatic rejection.

## 1. Understandable business and security

**Pass only if:** the company’s revenue engines, customers, unit economics, capital structure, security rights, principal competitors, and two largest failure modes can be explained plainly and supported by current primary sources.

**Automatic rejection:** opaque economics; misunderstood security terms; unaudited or materially unreliable reporting; thesis depends on rumor, promotion, or a business Joe cannot explain after review.

## 2. Demonstrated quality and financial resilience

**Pass only if:** evidence supports durable demand or advantage, credible management and governance, adequate liquidity through the horizon, and financial resilience appropriate to the industry. Evaluate revenue quality, margins, cash conversion, dilution, debt maturities, and return on capital; do not require one universal numeric threshold across industries.

**Automatic rejection:** going-concern warning; an unaddressed liquidity or refinancing cliff inside the thesis horizon; chronic dilution essential to survival; unresolved material reporting weakness or auditor issue; management credibility failure material to the thesis.

## 3. Valuation with asymmetric 3–6-month reward

**Pass only if:** a transparent valuation using relevant peers and/or fundamental scenarios supports a favorable probability-weighted return after realistic downside, dilution, taxes, and opportunity cost. State assumptions, current data, bear/base/bull values, probabilities, and sensitivity.

**Automatic rejection:** no defensible valuation; upside depends primarily on multiple expansion without fundamental support; base-case upside does not exceed bear-case loss; assumptions cannot be reconciled to primary-source figures.

## 4. Evidence-backed catalyst and falsifiable thesis

**Pass only if:** at least one plausible catalyst can affect value within 3–6 months, with a dated or observable pathway, while explicit invalidation conditions permit a pre-mortem and later audit. Distinguish a catalyst from a hoped-for price move.

**Automatic rejection:** no horizon-relevant catalyst; binary outcome with unbounded or unmeasured downside; thesis cannot be falsified; known event risk is omitted; catalyst is already fully reflected without a differentiated evidence-based view.

## 5. Portfolio fit and executable risk control

**Pass only if:** the proposed weight complies with every risk limit, preserves the cash floor, does not create prohibited theme or factor concentration, has sufficient liquidity, includes an entry range and exit/invalidation plan, and is superior to cash and the benchmark on a risk-adjusted basis.

**Automatic rejection:** any risk-limit breach; missing current-portfolio data; missing thesis-ledger row; incomplete mandate; inability to estimate portfolio exposure; cooling period not completed; size justified by conviction rather than downside.

## Universal automatic-rejection criteria

Reject regardless of tenet scores when there is an unresolved conflict of interest, unverifiable material claim, illegal or restricted instrument, unavailable reliable market data, requested use of leverage/options contrary to the mandate, or pressure to bypass documentation, risk limits, or the 24-hour cooling rule. Record the rejection and reason in the thesis ledger.
